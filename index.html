Ads Order Tracker

Ads Order Tracker is an internal team tool for viewing Google Ads campaign performance alongside SHOPLINE order information.

What the application does

The application helps authorized team members review advertising metrics, compare historical snapshots, and monitor campaign changes across connected accounts and stores.

Google Ads access

Google Ads account access is used to retrieve authorized advertising data for internal reporting and monitoring. Access is limited to accounts that the connected Google user is permitted to access.

Who can use it

This application is intended for authorized internal team members, not the general public.
