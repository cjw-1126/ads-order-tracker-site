Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

About Ads Order Tracker

Ads Order Tracker is an internal team application that helps authorized users review Google Ads campaign performance and SHOPLINE order information.

Information We Access

With authorization, the application accesses Google Ads account and campaign information, including performance metrics and campaign change information. It also processes SHOPLINE order information for internal reporting.

How We Use Information

Information is used to generate internal reports, historical snapshots, campaign comparisons, and operational monitoring for authorized team members.

Storage and Security

Application records and historical snapshots are stored on a locally operated Windows computer. Access to the application is intended to be restricted to authorized team members. The application uses authentication credentials to access connected services.

Data Sharing

The application is intended for internal business use. Information is made available to authorized team members for reporting and operational purposes. Any additional external data sharing must be disclosed in this policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application records may be deleted by the application operator. Users may contact the application operator to request information about access, retention, or deletion of their data.

Google API Data

Google Ads data is used for the internal advertising reporting and monitoring purposes described above. The application does not use Google Ads data for unrelated advertising profiling.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or data practices.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the application operator through the support contact provided on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
